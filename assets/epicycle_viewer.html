Reference values for κ/Ω:

• κ/Ω = 1.00 — Keplerian potential (closed elliptical orbits)

• κ/Ω = 1.41 — flat rotation curve (≈ Milky Way disk, default)

• κ/Ω = 2.00 — solid-body rotation (epicycle becomes a circle)

In the rotating frame, the epicycle is elongated tangentially with axis ratio 2Ω/κ, and the star traces it retrograde — opposite to the galaxy's rotation. The pericenter–apocenter precession rate (the foundation of Lin-Shu density wave theory) is Ω − κ/2.
